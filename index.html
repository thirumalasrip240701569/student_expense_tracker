    How totals are calculated

    • Total balance = all income − all expenses.
    • Savings = the amount you have set aside for your goal. It is part of your balance and can never be more than it.
    • Free to spend = total balance − savings.
    • Net savings (Reports) = income − expenses for the dates you choose. It describes a period, while Savings describes what you have set aside.
